Welcome !

You are crossing the line that separates humans from obedience to authority and entering a society of higher-order and elite civilisation, composed of intelligent beings, disciplined, elite minded, self-governed and committed innovators.

A step into this society is the exit from the lower circle of the world systems of human beings and the entrance into the higher systems of intelligent beings inspired by the astral higher civilisation. Here, we set and align with our goals, eliminate contrast and create our desired lifestyle.

Our social life holds no judgement, no hierarchy, no commands and no distinguished social class, for consciousness puts us on the same line.

Who Are the Mensan Elites Society?

The Mensan Elites Society is a community of disciplined, self-governed thinkers committed to learning, creativity, and meaningful contribution.

The Mensan Elites Institute

The Institute is our center for research, discussion, and shared knowledge. Members explore ideas across disciplines and turn rigorous thought into useful work.

Our Vision and Goal

We aim to build a culture where intelligence is guided by responsibility, collaboration, and a steady commitment to improving the systems around us.
